## Exact counterexamples to shortcuts

- `(0 mod 6)` alone cannot distinguish 0 and 6.
- CRT mod 6 and mod 9 has 18 distinct consistent states rather than 54 independent states.
- Same residue ticket recurs after LCM; e.g., n and n+1197 have the same (mod7,mod9,mod19) ticket.
- A 3-4-5 reflection sends `(1/25,0)` to `(7/625,24/625)` in a fixed Cartesian frame. No free denominator-25 closure.
- Numbers 25 and 35 lie in the 6k±1 candidate classes but are not prime.
- A modular chess move signature cannot determine tablebase outcome without enough remaining state and the legal game rules.

## Falsification policy

A negative test that begins with an exact source and stated predictions is an evidence asset. Store it next to the original load, emit a tri, and provide a next-best prediction. If the negative result is a counterexample to a universal conjecture, that version of the conjecture is false; the research branch remains open to a corrected proposition. No implicit overwrites.
