## What holds

Netherlands is the right blonde seat for **average** height, not for 6'10". Dutch women lead the world near 170 cm (about 5'7"), with Montenegro close behind. Baltic and Dinaric women sit in the same band. NCD-RisC / Landgeist 2024.

Africa is not the tall-mean seat. Published country means for women run shorter (South Africa ~159 cm, Nigeria ~158 cm, Ethiopia ~156 cm). A few East African pastoral groups are a known long-limb exception. That is a proportion seat, not a 6'10" class.

Two structures, not one:

1. **Pigment structure.** Eumelanin in the epidermis. Cutaneous melanoma is highest in fair-skinned, high-UV places (Australia, Northern Europe including the Netherlands) and much lower where eumelanin is high. South African Black incidence of cutaneous melanoma is on the order of one tenth to one twentieth of White incidence in the same sun. NCBI NBK481848. That protection fails in African albinism, which is the control: same latitude, little melanin, high skin cancer. Pigment is the structure. Height is not.
2. **Limb structure.** Sitting-height ratio. Relatively longer legs in many sub-Saharan and Australian series. Relatively shorter legs in some East Asian and Andean series. Dutch charts exist for sitting height and leg length (Fredriks 2005, ~14,500 Dutch children). Black South African series show longer limbs and longer distal limbs than White South African series. Soft-tissue correction for stature is population- and sex-specific. Do not reuse one formula on every skeleton.

6'10" cannot fill 1,000 public women. 6'3" (190.5 cm) is already several standard deviations above a ~162 cm female mean. A named list of ten is a celebrity list, not a sample.

To clear 1,000, the wiki gate drops to published **population structure**, which already clears 1,000 many times:
