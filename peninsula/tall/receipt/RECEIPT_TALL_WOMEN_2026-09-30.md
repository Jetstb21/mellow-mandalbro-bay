## Answer

Height in the ordinary range is mostly polygenic: many small variants, growth plate as the shared site. Extreme tall stature is a different seat. It can still be familial. It can also be a single-gene or endocrine carrier (Marfan / FBN1, GH excess, rarer gigantism genes). Same word "tall" is not the same carrier.

6'3" is already far above the US adult-woman mean (~5'4"). 6'9" is a probe. 6'10"+ is rare enough that public pages stop being population tips and start being "see a clinician for overgrowth" pages. This island does not invent a headcount for 6'10" women.

## Held (public, not ours)

- Polygenic height is saturated in European-ancestry GWAS. Genes converge on the growth plate. Nature Reviews Genetics, 2025. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41576-025-00834-1
- Genetically predicted height links both ways: lower risk for some cardiometabolic traits, higher risk for some cancers and atrial fibrillation. Not a personal forecast. EurekAlert, 4 Mar 2025. https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1075670
- Marfan: FBN1, connective tissue, often tall and thin, 50% chance if a parent carries the variant. Aorta is the load-bearing risk, including pregnancy and the year after. Mayo. https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/marfan-syndrome/symptoms-causes/syc-20350782
- Gigantism / acromegaly: usually GH-secreting pituitary tumor. X-linked acrogigantism (GPR101) is a rare childhood-onset cause with female predominance in the published cases. PMC8036715.
- High-dose estrogen to cap height in tall girls has a reported fertility and cancer downside. Marfan Trust note, 2021. https://www.marfantrust.org/articles/how-tall-is-too-tall-treatment-for-excessive-stature

## Basic tips (wiki, not orders)

- Shoes, bed, car seat, desk: fit the body. Pain often starts at the furniture, not the gene.
- Spine, knees, veins: tall length is a longer lever. Strength and shoes matter more than a supplement.
- Heart and eyes if the build is also thin, long-fingered, or there is a family aorta story. That is the Marfan seat, not the polygenic seat.
- Periods and fertility: ordinary tall stature is not infertility. Pregnancy risk jumps if a connective-tissue or aorta diagnosis is already on the table.
- Do not treat 6'10" as a lifestyle blog. That seat gets a medical workup before a wiki tip.

## DNA, short

- Common height: thousands of variants, additive.
- Rare tall: one gene or one hormone axis can dominate.
- Reproductive pass-through: Marfan variant is autosomal dominant (about 1 in 2). Most polygenic height is not a single coin-flip.
- This repo does not store genomes.
