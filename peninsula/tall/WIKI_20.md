Filter 6'3". Probe 6'9". Focus 6'10"+.
Answers are public-map, not exams.

1. Where does the filter sit? 6'3" = 190.5 cm. Above ordinary adult-woman height.
2. Where is the probe? 6'9" = 205.7 cm. Still public-rare. Not a separate disease.
3. Where is the focus? 6'10"+ = 208.3 cm+. Population pages thin out. Clinical pages start.
4. Is height one gene? Usually no. Polygenic. Growth plate is the shared tissue.
5. When is it one gene? Overgrowth syndromes. FBN1 / Marfan is the common named one.
6. When is it a hormone? GH excess. Pituitary tumor. Childhood gigantism, adult acromegaly.
7. What is X-linked acrogigantism? GPR101 duplication. Rare. Early childhood. Female-leaning in case series.
8. Does tall mean Marfan? No. Most tall women are familial tall stature.
9. What basic tip holds at 6'3"? Fit the world to the body: bed, car, shoes, lifting.
10. What basic tip holds at 6'10"? Stop at tips. Get a workup for overgrowth and aorta before lifestyle copy.
11. Heart? Ordinary tall: some lower coronary risk in studies, higher atrial fibrillation signal in genetic studies. Marfan: aorta is the point.
12. Cancer? Taller stature tracks higher risk for some cancers in epidemiology. Not a personal sentence.
13. Back and joints? Longer lever. Disc and knee load show up in tall cohorts. Shoes and strength, not a pill.
14. Veins? Height is a known venous-thromboembolism correlate in population data. Not a diagnosis.
15. Periods? Familial tall stature does not equal absent periods. Lost periods are their own inquiry.
16. Fertility? Ordinary tall is compatible with pregnancy. Old high-dose estrogen used to cap girls' height has a reported fertility cost.
17. Pregnancy at a Marfan seat? Aorta risk rises in pregnancy and can persist up to a year postpartum. Specialist, not a wiki.
18. What passes to a child? Polygenic height: tendency, not a switch. Marfan variant: about 1 in 2 if the parent carries it.
19. What did the team folder hold? Nothing on this seat. Check-in 018 is a different carrier.
20. What does this peninsula refuse? Patient data, treatment claims, welding a paper's height odds to a GRECO tick.
