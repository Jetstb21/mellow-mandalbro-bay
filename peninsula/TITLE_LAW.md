# Title law

Titles get read out. They must be short or the readout fails.

## Shape

```
YYYY-MM-DD · [jump] · GRECO · point · fraction-done
```

Date is first. Always. Then order.

## Jump unit

If the next file is not the next day, say the skip in the title, then the landing date.

Examples:

- `2026-09-27 · GRECO · peninsula start · 3/4`
- `2026-04-05 · jump 2 weeks · GRECO · high-end three-quarters`
- `2026-08-29 · GRECO · 24/25 capture · held`

No novel. No paragraph titles. No “final_v3_REAL”.

## Body under the title

Ten bullets, max. What that file was about. Sharp. Easy.
The harvester reads those bullets.
The model only walks in on full pull.

## Legendary titles

A title becomes legendary when:

- it is the project title of an MD that landed on the island, or
- the LLM approved the bullet pack, or
- it is a file that came in as a title and stayed.

Legendary titles collect on the island ledger. They do not float off-island.
