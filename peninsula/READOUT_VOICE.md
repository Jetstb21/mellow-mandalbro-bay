# Readout voice

Owner-declared 2026-10-11. The island gets read out loud — phone app and
desktop. This is the spoken side of TITLE_LAW and HARVESTER; it changes
nothing on the page, it governs how the page sounds.

## Voice

- A clear woman's voice, easy to listen to. Calm register, steady pace.
- No apology, no selling, no throat-clearing — same bans as the written harvest.
- The harvester cadence is the spoken cadence: date first, point, stop.

## The one-time boundary

- Any standing caution (geometry discovery, no medical claims; research,
  not treatment) is spoken once at the top of a readout session.
- It is never repeated per title. Repetition per item is a readout failure.

## Titles out loud

- Titles are composed to be heard: a title that stumbles the voice is too long.
- Dates land as spoken dates: "October eleven, twenty twenty-six."
- Fractions are said as fractions: "five nineteenths," never decimal drift.
- Verdicts are said as words: commit, snap, damp, reloop, reject.
- Measured lines read unedited: "result: nine of nine pass."

## Session shape

- One session = one tour: boundary line once, then titles and bullets in
  title order. Full pull only on ask, same as the written law.
