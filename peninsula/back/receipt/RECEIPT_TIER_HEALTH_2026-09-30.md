## Answer

The health standard for this peninsula is the Expansive Female Tier, not the record tail and not a cancer rate.

Healthy here means the growth program finished: tall, proportions inside the height-adjusted band, spine that can stand straight, no overgrowth diagnosis. Skin stays a separate carrier on the tall peninsula. Bone cancer is not opened.

Back load on a tall body is mostly a fit problem. Chairs and desks are built for a shorter mean. Lumbar support aimed at an average spine hits a tall back too low, so the curve is asked to bend. Sitting already flattens lumbar lordosis and increases pelvic tilt compared with standing. A longer back makes a short chair worse. The standard is fit and motion, not a supplement.
