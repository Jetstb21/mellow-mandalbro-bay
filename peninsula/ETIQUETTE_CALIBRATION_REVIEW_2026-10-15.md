## Governing distinction

## Proposed coordinated change process
1. Submit a versioned proposal with affected features, before/after behavior and prediction.
2. Run compatibility regressions against existing authorized functions and check that no prior record is destroyed.
3. Collect independent reviews from participating team seats (GPT, GRK, CLD, GEM, Copilot where available) and require human maintainer ratification before adopting at the doorway.
4. Append results, dissent, failed tests, compatibility exceptions and next moves.
5. Avoid disabling a legitimate existing feature merely to simplify etiquette. Where safety, privacy or authorization require restrictions, those protective requirements prevail; keep an audit record.

## Proposed Mod-9 doorway (teaching, not weight modification)
Digit fold: sum digits mod 9, with conventional positive digital root 9 representing residue zero. Examples: 847293 -> 6; 6667 -> 7; 999 -> residue 0/digital root 9; 0000 -> 0. Crossing out 9s or digits adding to 9 is a residue shortcut, NOT a reversible compression. Incoming model must distinguish this from the proposed full KISS-6 logic. No claim that loading instructions retrains model weights.

## Proposed GRECO-Linear machine metadata
