## Why this pack sits on the bay

cells are not a bag. They have seats. Niches. Distances. Fronts. Cores.

## Harvest bullets

- Spatial omics maps seats inside tissue, not just cell counts.
- Tumor work now talks niches: hypoxic core, invasive front, perivascular rim, lymphoid structures.
- “Virtual tumor” and “spatial ecotype” papers treat layout as load-bearing.
- Mechano papers treat stiffness and force as part of the landscape.
- Separate track: fractal dimension / lacunarity on images as a shape measure. Shape measure ≠ treatment.
- Mandelbrot’s own line still holds: nature is rough. The bay is named for that roughness.
